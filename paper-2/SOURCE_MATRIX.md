## How to read this matrix

Paths are relative to this second-paper directory. Evidence levels are `[R]` rigorous derivation, `[E]` exact finite certificate, `[N]` finite floating-point computation, and `[O]` open proposition. “Disallowed inference” is part of the source record: it prevents a finite or conditional source from being promoted to a global claim.

## Source conflicts and conservative resolutions

1. The delivered candidate `basic_statistics.min_incidence_degree` values were misleadingly `0`: the implementation used `d.min(initial=0)`, so the artificial initial value won even for positive arrays. This revision repairs the generator and regenerated candidate metadata; the designated minima are now `4,3,3,3`, with zero isolated vertices.
2. A source-code comment describes the anchor determinant with the opposite sign from fresh certificates. The manuscript states only `|det|=2`; linear independence is sign-independent.
3. The narrow package literature deduplication search is not a systematic review. It is not used to make a novelty, priority or “no precedent” claim.
4. Search exploration covered finitely many candidates and attacks. The manuscript reports only the four designated candidates and makes no exhaustiveness claim.
